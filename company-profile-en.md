# Quanzhou Weiliang Sporting Goods Co., Ltd. — Company Profile

> Public-facing profile. Suitable for sending to overseas buyers, publishing on the website, or attaching to enquiries.

## 1. Company Overview

Quanzhou Weiliang Sporting Goods Co., Ltd. is a factory-direct footwear manufacturer based in Jinjiang, Quanzhou, Fujian, China — one of China's main production hubs for sports and outdoor footwear. Our factory and export team operate within the same organisation, so development, sampling, mass production and shipment are handled by one team with fewer hand-offs.

Related entity: Quanzhou Zhina Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd. (same legal representative), handling export operations.

Brand: **FFBLWEI**, registered as a trademark in both China and the United States.

Positioning: a source factory supplying overseas wholesalers, footwear importers, outdoor retail chains, cross-border sellers and independent-store sellers. OEM/ODM and customisation supported.

## 2. Product Lines

We focus on two directions and three product types. Each line is developed for its own use case, not sold with one shared pitch.

### Hiking & Trekking Shoes

Built for mid- to long-distance hiking and mountain walking. The balance is support versus weight: ankle support structure, slip-resistant and abrasion-resistant outsole, lightweight upper for reduced fatigue over long walks.

### Water Shoes / Aqua Shoes / Beach Shoes

Built for streams, coastline and wet environments. The core is drainage and grip on wet surfaces: quick-draining construction, slip-resistant outsole, breathable stretch fit, easy to clean. Beach models are light and easy to slip on; stream-crossing models lean toward grip and protection.

### Indoor Yoga & Pilates Training Shoes

Built for yoga studios, Pilates studios and home training. Lightweight and close-fitting, quiet non-slip sole, breathable and non-stuffy, easy to pack and carry. A good fit for studio purchasing and training-gear channels.

## 4. How We Work With Buyers

- **OEM / ODM**: develop from your designs, samples or drawings, or adjust colourways, materials, outsoles and details on existing models.
- **Customisation**: logo, shoe box, hangtag, packaging and shipping marks.
- **MOQ**: tiered by model and material; small trial orders can be placed before scaling up.
- **Sampling**: samples can be made first to confirm feel, fit and colourways. Sample and freight costs are quoted separately.
- **Terms**: FOB Jinjiang; other terms quoted on request.
